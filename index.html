Everything you need to study, built into the browser

These features are already working in the current build.

A timer that fits how you work

Use Pomodoro or a free stopwatch. Set your own focus and break lengths, say what you’re working on before you start, and rate your focus in one tap afterwards. Between rounds, the break coach suggests a stretch, a glass of water, or the 20-20-20 eye rule.

Streaks that forgive

Rest-day tokens keep your streak alive after a missed day, so one bad day never erases weeks of work.

A heatmap of your effort

A 12-week calendar grid shows your study time. Darker tiles mean deeper sessions.

Exam countdowns

Add an exam date and see the days left, plus a daily pace to aim for. Hootly suggests a goal from your recent weeks.

Quests, XP and an owl that grows

Daily and weekly quests earn bonus XP. Level up to new ranks and unlock owl accessories.

Gentle focus help

Jot stray thoughts into the parking lot with Ctrl+Shift+P instead of leaving the page. Nudges are playful and you can snooze them. Nothing is ever blocked.

Study tools in one place

Open the command palette with Ctrl+K, copy citations in APA, MLA or BibTeX, play offline ambient sounds, and read with a focus ruler.

Make it yours, and make it easy on the eyes

Hootly ships with 16 themes: eight colorful gradients and eight inspired by the careers students are working toward. Every theme has a light and a dark mode, and the owl wears the colors. Profession themes add a small accessory, like a stethoscope or a gavel.

Try the theme dots at the top of this page.

  • Gradient themesAurora, Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Sakura, Lavender, Midnight and Mono Focus.
  • Profession themesMedical, Law, Engineering, Tech, Teaching, Architecture, Science and Business, each with its own rank names and greeting.
  • Comfortable by designDyslexia-friendly font option, high contrast modes, full keyboard control, and reduced motion support. Confetti and animations can be turned off.

Your study data stays on your computer

Many students are under 18, so privacy comes first. Hootly keeps everything in a database on your own device and sends nothing out by default.

  • Local firstSessions, streaks and XP live on your device.
  • No trackingNo telemetry, and no remote code running in the browser’s own screens.
  • Locked-down web pagesWebsites run in isolated, sandboxed views, kept apart from the app itself.
